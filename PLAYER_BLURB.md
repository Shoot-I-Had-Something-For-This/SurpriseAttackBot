## ⚡ Surprise Attack

Fast community score challenges. **One channel. Three modes.**

### How to enter

1. Wait for an event to go **LIVE** (bot posts in this channel)
2. Open the **scores thread** under that event (bot creates it automatically)
3. Play the announced song in **Arcade**, **Classic**, or **Fusion**
4. Use the **difficulty** shown on the LIVE post (if it says **Hardcore only**, play Hardcore)
5. In the **thread**, submit a score **either** way:
   - Results → **Discord → Submit Score** → **forward** that message, **or**
   - Post a **full scoreboard screenshot** (score + mode visible)
6. Look for ✅

Leaderboards stay in the main channel. Only scores go in the thread.

### Boards

There is **one** live leaderboard with three sections:

- 🕹️ **Arcade**
- 🥁 **Classic**
- 🔥 **Fusion**

You don’t pick a mode channel — the bot reads your score and places you.

### Rules

- Best score per player **per mode** for that event
- Wrong song → ❌ (when a song filter is set)
- Wrong difficulty → ❌ (when the event is locked, e.g. Hardcore only)
- New event = fresh boards (previous results are archived)

Questions? Ask an SA Operator.
